## Hello! 🗺

I'm Alejandro, a researcher in AI/ML and data science, mainly for environmental studies and land-planning applications, currently based in London, United Kingdom.

I'm passionate about technological developments, especially those with social impact. 
I've contributed and led a variety of [multidisciplinary projects](projects.md) underpinned by data analysis. 
In my spare time I enjoy my family 👪, hiking 🏔, cycling 🚴, and testing coffee svarieties (as a proud representative of my home country, Colombia 🇨🇴).

I hold a PhD in Physical Geography and an MSc in Environmental Modelling, Monitoring and Management from [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/geography/). 
Working with researchers at the [Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT](https://alliancebioversityciat.org/), during my postgraduate studies I built solid foundations in data science and large-scale cloud computing to propose methodologies for characterising the spatial and temporal drivers of deforestation in the Amazon biome using satellite products and geospatial information.

I'm affiliated with [The Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/) as a Research Fellow (April 2023 – present). 
Thanks to the support of a diverse team of professionals on my [team](https://www.turing.ac.uk/science-innovation/environment-and-sustainability/environmental-forecasting), the institute and the communities I belong to, I've been able to focus on developing open-source software and tools for the environmental sciences. 
I'm also training in open-science leadership, sharing my experiences through my blog and social media.

You can read more about my contributions, education and talks in my [Curriculum Vitae](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1PliksdlQhq3cscID0YlJfwVvABiifjFz?usp=sharing).
